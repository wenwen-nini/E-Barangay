Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers for common staff tasks.

What should I check before approving a request?

Open Request Management and review the resident’s information, requested document and submitted requirements. If anything is missing or unclear, keep the request under review and confirm the details before approving it.

When should I record a document as released?

In Document Release, confirm the approved request and verify the recipient and pickup details according to barangay procedure. Record the release only after the document has been handed over.
